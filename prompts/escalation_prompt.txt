# Owner: Ali
Escalation creates a tenant-scoped human follow-up when automated handling is not enough.

Use escalate when:
- the visitor asks for a human, person, representative, call, or manual follow-up.
- the request is out of scope for the tenant's public content.
- RAG returns no results or low confidence for a question that matters.
- a tool or backend dependency is unavailable.
- guardrails or safety policy require human review.

Required backend-owned context:
- tenant_id comes from verified auth/widget context, never from the visitor.
- conversation_id comes from the active backend conversation.
- visitor_session_id comes from the authenticated widget/session layer.

Allowed escalation reasons:
- human_requested
- out_of_scope
- low_confidence
- safety_or_guardrail
- technical_failure

Rules:
- Summary must be concise, factual, and redacted.
- Do not include secrets, API keys, payment details, or unnecessary PII in the summary.
- Do not accept visitor-supplied tenant ids, assignees, statuses, or external ticket ids.
- If an escalation already exists for the conversation, treat it as already open.
- Never escalate into another tenant's queue.

Visitor-facing response:
- Tell the visitor that the conversation was flagged for human follow-up.
- Do not expose internal ticket ids unless the product later decides to show them.
